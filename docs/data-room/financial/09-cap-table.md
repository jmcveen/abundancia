## 6. Distribution Waterfall

### 6.1 Waterfall Structure (Phase-1 Offering — subject to final documentation)

| Tier | Threshold | GP Promote | Description |
|------|-----------|-----------:|-------------|
| **Preferred Return & Return of Capital** | Until the 10% cumulative LP preferred is current and capital returned | 0% | 100% to the equity class — LP-class first (pref + capital before any promote) |
| **Tier 1** | LP IRR below 12% | 10% | First promote tier |
| **Tier 2** | LP IRR 12% – 15% | 20% | Second promote tier |
| **Tier 3** | LP IRR 15% – 18% | 30% | Third promote tier |
| **Tier 4** | LP IRR above 18% | 40% (uncapped) | Full promote tier |

*GP catch-up applies. Hold period: 10 years.*

### 6.2 Distribution Reserve

A **20% distribution reserve** is withheld from positive cash flows annually as a safety buffer and released in Year 10.

### 6.3 Waterfall Mechanics — Calculation Flow

```
Step 1: Determine distributable cash for the period
Step 2: Withhold 20% distribution reserve (annual safety buffer; released Year 10)
Step 3: Pay accrued 10% cumulative preferred return and return of capital
        (100% to the equity class — LP-class first)

Then, on remaining amounts (with GP catch-up):
  → Tier 1: 10% GP promote below 12% LP IRR
  → Tier 2: 20% GP promote in the 12–15% LP IRR band
  → Tier 3: 30% GP promote in the 15–18% LP IRR band
  → Tier 4: 40% GP promote above 18% LP IRR (uncapped)
```
