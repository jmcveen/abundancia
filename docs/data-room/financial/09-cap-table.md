## 6. Distribution Waterfall

### 6.1 Waterfall Structure (subject to final documentation)

| Tier | Threshold | LP Share | GP Share | Description |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Preferred Return** | Until 10% cumulative LP preferred is current | 100% | 0% | 10% cumulative preferred return on LP capital |
| **Tier 1** | Above preferred, up to 12% LP IRR | 90% | 10% | First promote tier |
| **Tier 2** | Above 12% LP IRR, up to 15% LP IRR | 80% | 20% | Second promote tier |
| **Tier 3** | Above 15% LP IRR | 60% | 40% | Full promote tier |

### 6.2 Distribution Reserve

A **10% distribution reserve** is withheld from positive cash flows for working capital before distributions are made.

### 6.3 Waterfall Mechanics — Calculation Flow

```
Step 1: Determine distributable cash for the period
Step 2: Withhold 10% distribution reserve for working capital
Step 3: Pay accrued 10% cumulative LP preferred return (100% to Class A)

Then, on remaining amounts:
  → Tier 1: 90% LP / 10% GP until LP IRR reaches 12%
  → Tier 2: 80% LP / 20% GP until LP IRR reaches 15%
  → Tier 3: 60% LP / 40% GP above 15% LP IRR
```
