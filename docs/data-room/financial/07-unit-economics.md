## A. The Offering — Phase-1 Unit Economics

**The investment offered is Phase 1 only.** Phase 1 sells **micro-villas and residential lots** — no houses, luxury estates, or condominiums are sold in Phase 1 — and operates a rental/hospitality and commercial program over the 10-year hold. Per the Phase-1 financial models, 10-year Phase-1 revenue is **$63,557,464**, identical under both offering Elections (Document 34):

| Phase-1 Product | Program | Revenue |
|-----------------|---------|--------:|
| **Micro-Villa Sales** | 60 micro-villas sold in Year 2 | $8,491,200 (Year 2) |
| **Residential Lot Sales** | 80 residential lots sold Years 2–3 (100 lots are prepared during Phase 1; 80 are sold within the Phase-1 model) | $10,817,449 (Year 2 $5,299,483 + Year 3 $5,517,966) |
| **Rentals, Hospitality & Commercial (combined)** | 50 short-term nature stays plus other STR units, 6BR Rental/Event House, Longevity Center & Spa, Community Center-anchored commercial and amenity operations | $44,248,815 (10-year; sub-stream split pending [TO CONFIRM]) |
| **Total Phase-1 Revenue (10-yr)** | | **$63,557,464** |

**Phase-1 micro-villa pricing:** ~$437/SF (avg $141,520 per unit) per the model's per-type schedule. **Phase-1 lot sales:** $10,817,449 across 80 residential lots (implied average ~$135K). Never presented as "houses" or "condos" — those products belong to later phases (context below).

The Phase-1 unit program supports the offering economics presented in Documents 06 and 09: 10-year Phase-1 EBITDA $23,413,145 (identical under both Elections) and LP returns of 26.02% IRR / 2.449x (Election A, 10-yr) or 34.81% IRR / 3.655x (Election B, 10-yr) on the $15,939,072 Phase-1 capitalization. See Document 33 (Key Figures Sheet) for the canonical reference.

## 11. Margin Sensitivity by Unit Type

Per-type margin sensitivity grids (price/cost stress by unit type) are being re-run on the September 2026 model and are [TO CONFIRM]. Project-level sensitivity is presented in Document 08, where the base case is the Phase-1 offering itself (26.02% LP IRR / 2.449x under Election A, or 34.81% / 3.655x under Election B) — the offering does not rely on the later phases described in this context section.

Directional observations pending the re-run:

- **Estate lots** carry the widest margin of safety (infrastructure-only cost basis vs. ~$150K average pricing) and close early (2028–2029)
- **Hempcrete construction** reduces operating cost exposure for buyers (50–70% HVAC/energy reduction vs. code baseline), supporting price resilience
- **Diversified streams** (residential 74.0%, rentals 17.5%, lots 4.7%, commercial 3.9%) plus a 10% project contingency ($22,046,302) buffer segment-level stress
