## 2. Interest

- **Rate:** 10% per annum, interest-only.
- **Basis:** Computed on the basis of a 360-day year.
- **Deferral:** Interest accrues from the date of this Note but payment is deferred during the Construction Period.
- **First Payment:** The first interest payment is due in Month 13 following the date of this Note, with payments continuing thereafter per the payment schedule delivered by the Borrower.

## 3. Maturity Date and Balloon Payment

- **Target Maturity:** This Note matures, and all outstanding principal and accrued unpaid interest becomes due and payable in a single balloon payment, at the end of **Year 2** from the date of this Note.
- **Optional Extension:** The Borrower may, at its option and subject to the conditions below, extend the Maturity Date to the end of **Year 4**. **[CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL — extension conditions, notice requirements, and any extension fee or rate adjustment.]** Investors should note that an extension is projected to grow the outstanding balance to approximately $5.6M in aggregate across the Note series and requires the Borrower to suspend Years 2-3 distributions to LP equity holders to fund the extended obligation (see Document 31, Term Sheet).
- **No Acceleration by Election B Alone:** Nothing in this Note accelerates maturity solely because an investor has elected Election B; acceleration occurs only upon an Event of Default as described in Section 7.

## 4. Prepayment

## 6. Security

This Note is secured by a lien on the Property and related Collateral (as defined in the Deed of Trust, Document 32) granted by the Borrower to a trustee for the benefit of the Holder and the other holders of Notes in the series, pursuant to a Deed of Trust to be recorded in the Official Public Records of Bastrop County, Texas.

**Lien Priority — Subordination.** The lien securing this Note is, and at all times shall remain, **subordinate and junior** to the lien of the Borrower's construction lender securing the Phase-1 construction loan (currently anticipated at $7,700,000, 8.5%, 10-year fully amortizing). The Holder acknowledges that in the event of a default, foreclosure, or liquidation, the construction lender's lien will be satisfied first, and the Holder's recovery under this security is subject and subordinate to that prior lien. This subordination is a deliberate and material term of this Note and is not a drafting inadvertence. **[CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL — form and execution of a subordination/intercreditor agreement with the construction lender.]**

Until the Deed of Trust described above is executed and recorded, this Note is, notwithstanding its title, unsecured in practice. See Document 32 for current recording status.
