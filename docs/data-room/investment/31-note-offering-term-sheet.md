## 1. Overview

Abundancia Community LLC (the "Company") is offering a series of **Secured Promissory Notes** (the "Notes") in an aggregate principal amount of **$4,000,000**, as one of two available Elections for capitalizing the Phase-1 land tranche. Investors who elect Election B (see Document 34, Investor Election Notice) hold Notes instead of a proportional share of LP equity for that $4,000,000 portion of their investment. Note Holders are a distinct class of investor from LP equity holders: Notes pay fixed interest only and are **not** membership interests in the Company — Note Holders do not participate in project upside, distributions, or governance.

## 3. Security & Priority

The Notes are secured by a lien on the Property (the "Collateral") to be granted under a Deed of Trust (Document 32), recorded in the Official Public Records of Bastrop County, Texas.

**This security is explicitly subordinate.** The Notes' lien ranks **behind the Construction Lender's first-priority lien** on the Property ($7,700,000 at 8.5%, 10-year fully amortizing). Note Holders rank ahead of LP and GP equity in a liquidation, but behind the construction lender. This subordination is a deliberate structural fact, not an oversight — the construction loan is required to build the Phase-1 program, and lenders of that type require first-lien position as a condition of funding. Investors should read this term sheet, Document 30 (Form of Note), Document 32 (Deed of Trust), and Document 16 (Risk Disclosure) together before electing Election B.

## 5. Risk Factors

**Subordination Risk.** The Notes are not first-lien secured debt. In a default, foreclosure, or liquidation scenario, the Construction Lender is paid first from Property proceeds; Note Holders are paid only from what remains, ahead of LP/GP equity but behind the construction lender. There is no guarantee that Property value net of the construction lien will be sufficient to fully repay the Notes.

**Balloon / Refinance Risk.** The Notes are structured to be repaid from Year-2 sale proceeds. If those proceeds are delayed or insufficient, the maturity may extend to Year 4, growing the balance to approximately $5.6M and requiring the Company to suspend LP distributions in Years 2-3 to service the extended balance.

**No Upside Participation.** Note Holders receive fixed 10% interest only. Unlike LP equity, Notes do not participate in project appreciation, promote, or any return above the stated rate — even if the underlying project substantially outperforms projections.

**Illiquidity.** The Notes are not registered securities and there is no public market for them. Note Holders should expect to hold to maturity.

**Unsecured Until Recorded.** Until the Deed of Trust is recorded (Document 32), the Notes are, notwithstanding their name, unsecured in practice. See Document 32 for current status.
