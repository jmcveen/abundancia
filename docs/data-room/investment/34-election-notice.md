## 1. What This Form Does

Phase 1 of the Abundancia offering is capitalized identically — $15,939,072 total, on identical Phase-1 EBITDA of $23,413,145 — under two available capital-structure **Elections** for the $4,000,000 land tranche. Every investor completing a Subscription Agreement (Document 04) must also complete this Election Notice, choosing **one** of the two Elections below. See Document 33 (Key Figures Sheet) for the full side-by-side terms and Document 31 (Term Sheet) for complete Note Holder detail if electing Election B.

## 2. Make Your Election (check one)

**☐ Election A — All-Equity**
LP equity funds the full $4,000,000 land tranche as ordinary Class A Units, sharing fully in project upside alongside the balance of my investment.
*Target 10-Yr LP IRR: 26.02% · Target 10-Yr LP Equity Multiple: 2.449x*

**☐ Election B — Secured Notes**
The $4,000,000 land tranche is instead funded by Secured Promissory Notes at 10% interest-only (Document 30), reducing the LP equity base and concentrating the equity return on the remainder. I understand and accept that these Notes are secured by a lien on the Property that is **subordinate to the Construction Lender's first-priority lien**, are subject to balloon/refinance risk at Year 2 (extendable to Year 4, with LP distributions suspended in Years 2-3 if extended), and pay fixed interest only with no upside participation.
*Target 10-Yr LP IRR: 34.81% · Target 10-Yr LP Equity Multiple: 3.655x*

## 3. What Differs Between the Elections

| | Election A | Election B |
|---|---|---|
| **Return Profile** | Lower target IRR, full upside participation on the entire investment | Higher target IRR on the equity portion; the note portion earns fixed interest only |
| **Note Subordination Risk** | None — no notes involved | Notes rank behind the construction lender; see Document 31 §5 |
| **Repayment Timing** | N/A — equity, held for the full 10-year term | Notes targeted for Year-2 balloon repayment, with Year-4 extension contingency |
| **Developer Fee Base** | 4% of full $15,939,072 capitalization | 4% of $11,939,072 (workbook's capitalization definition excluding the notes tranche) |

Investors who are uncertain which Election best fits their objectives and risk tolerance should consult their own financial and legal advisors before completing this form.
